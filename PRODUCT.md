# Product

## Platform

web

## Stack

Next.js App Router + TypeScript + Tailwind + Firebase (Firestore, Auth), from the team's build brief (`Urvara_AI_Claude_Code_Prompt-2.md`). Vite is explicitly excluded.

## Users

- **Primary audience right now: hackathon judges** (Hackoween). The product first has to land in a short demo or pitch.
- **Farm owners / landowners** managing several farms: they understand performance, approve schedule changes, and manage farmers, fields, tasks and shared resources (Owner Portal).
- **Farmers and farm workers**, including people with limited literacy or little experience with apps: they report problems and progress by voice, see today's work, and ask practical questions (Farmer Portal, mobile-first).

## Product Purpose

Urvara AI is a voice-first, AI-assisted platform for agricultural operations and coordination across several farms. Tagline: *Every Farm. Every Resource. In Sync.* ("Urvara" is Sanskrit for fertile land.)

- **Mechanism:** combines forecasts, local conditions, soil readings, crop-health reports, inventory, people, machines and schedules. It recommends and schedules actions and replans when conditions change, showing what changed, why, and what needs confirmation.
- **Success, for now:** a judge understands the product quickly, triggers a real incident, and watches Urvara coordinate the response across farms, with the reason for each decision.

## Positioning

Autonomous multi-farm coordination, not a monitoring dashboard or a chatbot. Deterministic, explainable replanning across shared machines, people and weather, with owner approval, plus a voice-first farmer experience in Hindi and English.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- **Multilingual:** Hindi and other regional Indian languages must be supported. Which languages, and how many at launch, is still open.
- **Data:** no real data or live APIs needed for the hackathon. Mock or sample data is acceptable, but it must be presented honestly as demo data.
- **Halloween theming:** not stated as a requirement. Do not assume it.

## Brand Commitments

- Product name: **Urvara**.
- Team name: **Laal Kabutar**, to be shown as the team or brand behind the product.

## Evidence on Hand

None yet: no testimonials, users, field results, partners or metrics. Do not invent yield gains, farmer counts, accuracy figures or endorsements.

## Product Principles

1. **Demo clarity first.** A judge should grasp the problem and what Urvara does within seconds.
2. **Language is part of the product, not an add-on.** Hindi and regional-language support is designed in from the start.
3. **Honest about data.** Mock data is clearly sample data. No made-up impact claims.
